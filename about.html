Who we are

Business Outcome Assurance for the AI era.

Qapitol is the AI control layer that sits between your AI ambitions and the outcomes you’ve promised your business. We evaluate it, govern it, secure it, and assure that it delivers — continuously, in production, with evidence your auditors can read.

We’ve spent close to a decade working alongside engineering teams at India’s most regulated enterprises and fastest-growing unicorns. What we learned: quality is only valuable when it’s connected to a business outcome. A test that doesn’t prevent a bad AI response isn’t a test — it’s theatre. A compliance audit that happens after deployment isn’t governance — it’s cleanup.

That conviction shapes everything we build. Our platforms — QAVE, Cheq, Nexus, Agent Fabric — deliver provable, measurable outcomes, not activity reports. Our SURE-Q Framework gives CIOs and compliance officers a structured path from AI ambition to AI assurance. Our talent and GCC services give global enterprises the engineering capacity to execute.

Vision

A world where enterprise AI is trusted, explainable, and continuously assured — not hoped for.

We are building toward a future where every AI system in production carries the same level of evidenced assurance that software quality engineers applied to critical releases a decade ago — except automated, continuous, and audit-ready by default.

Mission

To be the independent quality and governance layer every enterprise AI system needs.

We evaluate, govern, and assure AI outcomes at production scale — inside regulated environments, at real-world latency, with evidence your auditors, regulators, and board can rely on. Not after deployment. Continuously.

Values

Six commitments we don't negotiate on.

These aren't aspirational posters. They're the filters every product decision, engagement, and hire runs through.

📊

Outcomes over activity

We measure what shipped, what was caught, what was saved. Dashboards full of test counts and coverage percentages that don't connect to business outcomes are not our product.

⚖️

Independence as infrastructure

Our value, like a financial auditor's, depends entirely on independence. We are not incentivised to tell you your AI model is performing well. We tell you the truth, with evidence.

🎯

Depth over breadth

We'd rather be the definitive AI quality and governance layer for 25 regulated enterprises than a generic vendor to 250. Domain depth in BFSI, healthcare, and regulated AI is a competitive moat, not a constraint.

🏭

Production is the only stage that counts

AI that works in a demo is unremarkable. AI that works at production load, under regulatory scrutiny, across edge cases you didn't script — that's what we're hired to assure. Everything else is rehearsal.

🔄

Continuous over periodic

Quality assurance isn't an audit event scheduled for quarter-end. It's an operating condition — embedded in pipelines, running at inference time, alerting in real-time. Periodic reviews are compliance theatre.

🔬

Transparency in everything

With clients on what our platforms actually do. With partners on what's genuinely integrated vs. planned. With ourselves when something isn't working. Provable outcomes, not assurances. Evidence, not narratives.
