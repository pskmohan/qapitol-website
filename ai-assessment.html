✓ AI Readiness Assessment

Is your AI deployment
audit-ready?

10 questions · 5 minutes · Personalised score + action plan

📊 Governance posture 🛡️ Regulatory exposure ⚡ Operational readiness 🔍 Model evaluation maturity
Question 1 of 10
How many AI models or AI-powered features does your organisation currently have in production?
Include ML models, LLM integrations, recommendation engines, and AI-assisted workflows.
Question 2 of 10
Do you have a documented AI governance policy or responsible AI framework in place?
This could include an AI ethics policy, model risk management policy, or SURE-Q / ISO 42001 alignment.
Question 3 of 10
How do you currently test and validate your AI models before deploying to production?
Testing for hallucinations, bias, drift, adversarial inputs, and regulatory alignment.
Question 4 of 10
Are you subject to any AI-specific regulations or planning to operate in regulated markets?
RBI DPDP, EU AI Act, IRDAI, HIPAA/NHB, MeitY guidelines, or sector-specific AI mandates.
Question 5 of 10
Do you monitor your AI models in production for performance degradation, data drift, or unexpected behaviour?
Post-deployment monitoring is the most common gap in enterprise AI programmes.
Question 6 of 10
Can you produce an explainability report for any AI decision your system makes — on demand?
Regulators like RBI now require per-decision audit trails for credit-scoring AI within 24 hours.
Question 7 of 10
Have you conducted adversarial testing (red-teaming) on your LLMs or AI applications?
Including prompt injection, jailbreak attempts, OWASP LLM Top-10, and domain-specific attack vectors.
Question 8 of 10
Do you have clear data governance for the training data used by your AI models?
Data lineage, consent management, bias audits, and privacy-preserving practices.
Question 9 of 10
How is AI quality and governance responsibility structured in your organisation?
Clear ownership is the #1 predictor of audit success in enterprise AI programmes.
Question 10 of 10
If a regulator audited your AI systems today, how confident are you in passing?
Be honest — this determines your personalised action plan recommendations.
