What is AI TRiSM?

The analyst benchmark for enterprise AI risk management

Gartner published AI TRiSM — AI Trust, Risk and Security Management — as a Top Strategic Technology Trend. It defines four structural layers of AI risk that every enterprise must address before deploying AI at scale in regulated or customer-facing contexts.

The framework is technology-neutral: it doesn't tell you which vendor to buy. It tells boards, CISOs, and AI leads what categories of control they are accountable for. Most enterprises either buy a patchwork of point tools across all four layers — or skip entire layers because they don't know where to start.

Qapitol's AI TRiSM managed service removes that complexity. Rather than four vendor contracts and four integration projects, you get one partner, one SLA, and structured coverage from day one — delivered by a team that lives inside your AI risk programme, not a tool that sits outside it.

Source: Gartner, AI Trust, Risk and Security Management (AI TRiSM), 2024. Qapitol AI Technologies is not affiliated with or endorsed by Gartner, Inc. The Gartner name and AI TRiSM terminology are trademarks of Gartner, Inc. and/or its affiliates.
