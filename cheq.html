Why it exists

Compliance teams weren’t built for AI-speed regulation.

IRDAI released 47 new circulars in a single year. RBI data localisation rules keep shifting. The EU AI Act enforcement deadline doesn’t care about your backlog. And somewhere in your product portfolio, a policy document is already out of compliance — you just don’t know which one.

Manual review doesn’t scale. Generic AI tools don’t understand obligation structures. And a non-compliant policy that makes it to market in BFSI doesn’t get a second chance. Cheq closes that gap — by training purpose-built small language models on regulatory corpora and running automated compliance checks against every product document in your portfolio.

What Cheq does

Automated compliance checking. Structured. Continuous. Audit-ready.

Four layers that take you from regulatory text to defensible compliance evidence.

Obligation Library

Regulatory corpus ingestion

Cheq ingests regulatory frameworks — IRDAI Master Circular, Broker Regulations, RBI data localisation rules, EU AI Act articles — and converts them into structured Atomic Compliance Units (ACUs). Each ACU is an unambiguous, checkable obligation.

SLM Engine

Purpose-built compliance SLMs

Three small language models work together: an Insurance NER SLM that classifies policy document entities, a Complexity Classifier that flags high-risk provisions, and a Judge SLM that evaluates each clause against the relevant ACU. Trained on regulatory text, not generic corpora.

Policy Reasoning Traces

Audit-defensible evidence trail

Every compliance check produces a Policy Reasoning Trace (PRT) — a structured document showing which regulation was checked, which clause was assessed, what the model concluded, and why. PRTs are formatted for regulator submission and internal audit.

Compliance Dashboard

Portfolio-level visibility

A compliance analytics dashboard shows score trends, obligation gap heatmaps, and portfolio-level views across all your product documents. Scheduled re-checks trigger automatically when regulatory circulars update.

Architecture

Enterprise-first. Secure by design.

Cheq runs on-premise or in a dedicated cloud environment — no policy documents leave your perimeter. The production stack runs on Kubernetes with full RBAC, multi-tenancy, and audit logging.

Client onboarding is self-serve through a Next.js compliance portal. Each new tenant configures their regulatory jurisdiction, uploads product documents, and gets a compliance baseline within a single session.

Cheq integrates with document management systems (SharePoint, Confluence, Box) and can trigger compliance checks from your CI/CD pipeline when product documentation changes.
