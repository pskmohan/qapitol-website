Group 1 — Data & Privacy

Only the data you explicitly provide for evaluation or compliance testing. Qapitol operates a strict data minimisation policy.

For most engagements, we work with synthetic data or anonymised test data. If production data is required, it is processed under a signed DPA, encrypted in transit and at rest, and deleted within 30 days of engagement end.

VPC deployment option means your data never leaves your environment at all. We can run the full Qapitol platform stack inside your cloud account (AWS, Azure, or GCP) — in which case we have no access to your data at any point.

By default, Qapitol's SaaS platform runs on AWS ap-south-1 (Mumbai, India). All data is stored in-region. We do not transfer data across regions unless you explicitly configure cross-region replication in a VPC deployment.

For enterprises with data residency requirements, we offer VPC deployment inside your own cloud account — data never leaves your perimeter. On-premise deployment is available for air-gapped environments (Defence, Government, regulated BFSI).

No. Qapitol does not use client data to train, fine-tune, or improve any model — Qapitol's or third-party. This is not a policy statement that might change — it is contractually guaranteed in our MSA.

Our evaluation models run in isolated inference environments. Each client's data is processed independently with no cross-client model state. There is no feedback loop from your data to our model training pipelines.

Group 2 — Vendor Security Posture

ISO 27001:2022 certified — covering all Qapitol AI platform services and managed delivery operations.

SOC 2 Type II audit is in progress (target Q3 2026). You can request our current SOC 2 bridge letter, which outlines the controls in place during the audit period. Annual VAPT is conducted by an independent third party — executive summary available under NDA to reduce duplication with your own testing.

We notify affected clients within 4 hours of confirming a P1 incident — not after we've fully resolved it. You'll know what happened, what we know so far, what immediate containment actions we've taken, and when to expect our next update.

Resolution target is 24 hours for critical severity, 72 hours for high. Written root cause analysis is provided within 5 business days of resolution. Our full incident response runbook is available to enterprise clients on request.

Yes. Enterprise clients may conduct their own VAPT against Qapitol's hosted environments under a coordinated disclosure agreement. We will provide you with a test environment, IP ranges, and a designated point of contact from our security team.

We share our most recent third-party VAPT executive summary (under NDA) to help your team scope any additional testing and avoid duplicating coverage we've already done. Typical turnaround for coordinated testing arrangement: 5 business days to set up.

Group 3 — AI-Specific Risk

Qapitol's MSA includes an indemnification clause covering direct losses arising from platform errors within documented scope. Specific liability limits and carve-outs are detailed in the enterprise agreement — we are happy to walk your legal team through these.

We recommend — and our platform is designed to support — maintaining a human review layer for final regulatory decisions. Qapitol is built to augment, not replace, your compliance function. Our coverage maps to 1,400+ documented regulatory obligations; anything outside that documented scope is flagged rather than silently evaluated.

Every compliance check, evaluation score, and policy trace in CHEQ and QAVE produces an immutable audit log with full reasoning. These aren't black boxes.

Policy Reasoning Traces (PRTs) show exactly which regulatory clause was applied, which model was used, what the confidence threshold was, and why the output was classified as compliant or non-compliant. PRTs are formatted for regulator submission and are exportable in formats compatible with your GRC tooling (JSON, CSV, PDF). You can plug them directly into ServiceNow GRC, Splunk, or your SIEM of choice.

Our platforms are designed with explicit uncertainty thresholds. Outputs below a configurable confidence threshold are flagged for human review — they are never silently passed as compliant. The threshold is customer-configurable so you can make it as conservative as your risk appetite requires.

QAVE's evaluation suite includes adversarial and edge-case test coverage to surface failure modes before you reach production. We publish our known limitations for each compliance domain in our product documentation — we do not hide known gaps. If a regulatory domain is not in our obligation library, we say so.

Group 4 — Integration & Access

SSO via SAML 2.0 and OIDC — compatible with Okta, Azure AD, Ping Identity, and Google Workspace. If you run a different IdP, contact us — we can assess compatibility during the PoC.

Granular RBAC is configurable per platform. MFA is enforced for all users. Qapitol staff access to client environments is JIT (just-in-time), individually logged, and reviewed quarterly. No standing privileged access exists to any client environment.

Yes — and we recommend it. Standard enterprise onboarding starts with a 2-week sandbox PoC using synthetic or anonymised data. No production data is required to complete the PoC.

This gives your InfoSec team the time to complete vendor review while your technical and compliance teams are simultaneously validating fit. We provide a dedicated sandbox environment, test data templates, and a named technical contact. PoC environments are fully isolated from production infrastructure.

On contract termination, all client data is purged within 30 days. We provide a written confirmation of deletion, listing what was deleted and from which systems. This is a contractual commitment, not a best-effort target.

Before deletion, we provide you with exports of your configuration, audit logs, compliance evidence, and any Policy Reasoning Traces you haven't already exported — in standard formats (JSON, CSV, PDF). No data lock-in. You leave with everything you generated.

Trust Summary

What you hand over vs. what Qapitol guarantees.

What you hand over

  • → Anonymised or synthetic test data (recommended default)
  • → Production data only if required, under signed DPA
  • → System integration credentials (scoped to what we need)
  • → Regulatory scope and policy documents for compliance testing
  • → Access to a sandbox or staging environment for PoC

What Qapitol guarantees

  • ✓ No model training on your data — ever, contractually
  • ✓ Data deleted within 30 days of contract end, written confirmation provided
  • ✓ 4-hour notification SLA for P1 security incidents
  • ✓ JIT-only staff access, logged and reviewed quarterly
  • ✓ Immutable audit logs exportable in standard formats
  • ✓ VPC deployment option — zero data egress
  • ✓ ISO 27001:2022 certified across all platform services
  • ✓ Full data and config export before contract end — no lock-in
