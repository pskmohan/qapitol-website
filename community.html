The Vision

What the community will be

We're being deliberate about what we build here. Quality over quantity — a focused, practitioner-first community with real resources, not another newsletter or Slack ghost town.

Open Source

Open source tools from Qapitol Labs

We'll release the tools we actually use internally as open source — eval harnesses, prompt testing utilities, AI test data generators, and quality metric frameworks. Real tools, not demo projects. Built on GitHub, community-maintained.

  • Eval harness for LLM response quality
  • Prompt regression testing toolkit
  • AI test data schema validators
  • SURE-Q scoring utilities
Forum

Forum for QE & AI practitioners

A structured discussion forum — not a firehose group chat. Organized by topic: agentic testing, AI evaluation, governance & compliance, test data, career & leadership. Moderated to keep signal high and noise low.

  • Topic-organized threads, not a Slack flood
  • Expert answering from Qapitol team
  • Weekly "what's working" threads
  • AMA sessions with practitioners
Eval Frameworks

Shared eval frameworks

A curated library of evaluation frameworks for AI systems — contributed by practitioners and vetted by Qapitol. Covers LLM evals, agentic system testing, safety testing, and domain-specific benchmarks for BFSI, healthcare, and logistics.

  • Pre-built eval templates by domain
  • Community-contributed test suites
  • Versioned framework releases
  • Integration guides for major platforms
Learning

Certification study groups

Cohort-based study groups for QE and AI engineering certifications — ISTQB AI Testing, AWS ML Specialty, Google Cloud AI, and the emerging AI governance certifications. Peer-learning with structured weekly sessions and shared study materials.

  • ISTQB AI Testing syllabus coverage
  • AWS ML & Google Cloud AI cohorts
  • Shared flashcards & practice exams
  • Accountability check-ins

Preview

Three things to look forward to

Here's what we're most excited to ship first — based on what practitioners have told us they need most.

01

Qapitol Labs — First OSS Release

Our first open source tool will be an LLM eval harness — a lightweight Python framework for running structured evaluations against language model outputs. Supports custom scorers, batch runs, and integration with major model providers.

Python LLM Evals MIT License
02

Private Beta — Practitioner Forum

A curated group of 100 early waitlist members will get first access to our private beta forum. We'll run it for 60 days before opening to the wider community — this is your chance to shape the structure and community norms from the start.

100 Spots 60-day Beta Shape the Community
03

First Certification Study Cohort

We'll launch our first study cohort focused on ISTQB AI Testing Certification — a structured 6-week program with weekly sessions, shared study materials, practice exams, and a study buddy matching system. Free for community members.

ISTQB AI 6 Weeks Free
