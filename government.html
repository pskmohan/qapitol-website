THE ACCOUNTABILITY CHALLENGE

Why Government AI Needs a Different Standard

Public sector AI makes decisions that affect livelihoods, rights, and access to services. Standard commercial AI governance is not sufficient — citizens and oversight bodies demand more.

⚖️
RIGHTS RISK

Lack of Explainability for Citizen-Facing Decisions

When AI denies a subsidy, rejects a permit, or prioritises one beneficiary over another, citizens have a legal right to an explanation. Most deployed AI can't provide it.

💰
GOVERNANCE RISK

Procurement Fraud AI With Unacceptable False Positive Rates

AI fraud detection that flags legitimate vendors as suspicious disrupts critical public procurement, creates political risk, and requires costly manual reversal processes.

🔒
SECURITY RISK

Data Sovereignty & Security Requirements

Citizen data — Aadhaar-linked, health, tax, and welfare records — cannot be processed by foreign cloud AI services. Sovereign deployment with air-gap capability is non-negotiable.

📋
ACCOUNTABILITY GAP

Regulatory Accountability for AI Errors

When an AI-driven policy recommendation leads to harm, which department is accountable? Without documented model governance, ministries face parliamentary scrutiny without defence.

GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORKS

The Regulatory Landscape for Government AI

Public sector AI operates under a layered framework of MeitY guidelines, data protection law, procurement rules, and constitutional accountability requirements.

🇮🇳

MeitY AI Guidelines

National AI Policy

India's National AI Strategy and MeitY's responsible AI framework require high-risk public sector AI to undergo conformity assessment, bias evaluation, and documented governance.

🔒

DPDP Act 2023

Citizen Data Protection

AI processing Aadhaar-linked data, health records, tax information, or welfare entitlements must comply with DPDP consent, purpose limitation, and data principal rights.

🏛️

CAG & Parliamentary Scrutiny

Public Accountability

AI decisions affecting public expenditure, programme delivery, and citizen rights are subject to CAG audit and parliamentary committee scrutiny — requiring auditable evidence trails.

🔐

NCIIPC / CERT-In

Critical Infrastructure

Government AI connected to critical national infrastructure must meet NCIIPC security standards and CERT-In incident reporting requirements.

📋

CVC / Procurement Rules

Integrity & Compliance

AI in public procurement must comply with Central Vigilance Commission guidelines on integrity, fairness, and transparent decision-making in government contracting.

Citizens, courts, and parliamentary committees will demand accountability from government AI. Build with assurance from day one — not after the first controversy.
