Telecom, Media & Technology

Network AI that hallucinate
costs you coverage. Not credibility.

Telecom operators and media platforms are deploying AI across network operations (NetOps), customer experience, content moderation, and revenue assurance — at billions of transactions per day. At that scale, AI drift isn't a data science problem. It's a network reliability event. Qapitol builds the continuous evaluation and governance layer that keeps AI-driven telecom operations trusted at TRAI-scale volumes.

Where AI risk is highest in Telecom

  • →Network fault prediction — wrong predictions mean unnecessary outages or missed real failures. Precision/recall at 99.9% uptime standards.
  • →Content moderation AI — over-filtering damages user trust; under-filtering creates regulatory and brand exposure. Both need continuous calibration.
  • →Revenue assurance — AI-driven billing logic errors at scale mean undetected revenue leakage or fraudulent charge-backs. Silent drift at billion-transaction volumes.
  • →Churn prediction AI — models built on historical patterns degrade as market conditions shift. False negatives mean losing customers you could have saved.
  • →TRAI compliance — AI-driven customer care must meet TRAI's QoS benchmarks, DND compliance, and grievance redressal timelines. Automated evidence trail required.
