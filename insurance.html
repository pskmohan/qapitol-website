THE INSURANCE AI RISK REGISTER

Why Insurance AI Has a Governance Gap

Insurance AI makes decisions that affect people's financial security. Unexplainable models, biased underwriting, and hallucinating claims agents expose insurers to regulatory sanction, litigation, and reputational harm.

📋
REGULATORY RISK

Unexplainable Underwriting Decisions

IRDAI and consumer protection law require insurers to explain why a premium was priced or a risk declined. AI models that can't produce a plain-language rationale create legal exposure on every policy issued.

🔍
CX & COMPLIANCE RISK

Claims Fraud AI With High False Positive Rates

Aggressive fraud models that flag legitimate claims erode customer trust, create complaint volumes, and trigger IRDAI scrutiny — costing more than the fraud they prevent.

⚖️
FAIRNESS RISK

Biased Risk Scoring Across Customer Segments

Underwriting models trained on historical data can encode socioeconomic or geographic bias — systematically overcharging protected groups in ways that breach IRDAI fair practice requirements.

🤖
LIABILITY RISK

Hallucinating Claims Settlement Agents

AI agents handling claim queries, settlement calculation, or policy interpretation that produce incorrect outputs expose insurers to mis-selling liability and ombudsman complaints.

REGULATORY LANDSCAPE

The Compliance Frameworks Governing Insurance AI

Insurance AI sits at the intersection of IRDAI guidelines, data protection law, actuarial standards, and emerging global AI governance requirements.

🏛️

IRDAI AI Guidelines

Insurance Regulator

IRDAI's Technology Committee has flagged AI explainability, fair pricing, and data governance as priority areas. AI-driven underwriting and claims decisions must have documented rationale available on regulatory request.

🇮🇳

DPDP Act 2023

Data Protection

Policyholder health, financial, and behavioural data processed by AI must comply with DPDP consent, purpose limitation, and data principal rights — including automated decision-making provisions.

📊

Actuarial Practice Standards

APS / IAI

AI models used in reserving, pricing, and risk classification must meet Institute of Actuaries of India practice standards for model validation, documentation, and peer review.

🌍

Solvency II / IFRS 17

International Standards

Insurers with international operations or reinsurance arrangements face Solvency II model governance requirements — particularly for internal model approval and AI-assisted capital calculation.

⚖️

Consumer Protection Law

Fair Practice Code

IRDAI's Master Circular on Fair Practices requires that AI-driven sales, underwriting, and claims decisions do not disadvantage customers on grounds unrelated to genuine risk factors.

Your Insurance AI Needs to Be as Trustworthy as Your Policies

Every automated underwriting decision and AI-settled claim is a regulatory and liability event. Govern it before IRDAI, the ombudsman, or a court does.
