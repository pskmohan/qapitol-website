Common questions

FAQ

Do I need to be available full-time?+

No. QEN is designed for practitioners who are already working and want to contribute on a flexible basis. Most Tier 1 and Tier 2 tasks take 30 minutes to 3 hours. You pick up tasks when you're available. There are no minimums.

What if I'm currently employed — is this a conflict?+

Most employment contracts allow independent consulting on a non-competing basis. QEN tasks are typically in AI governance and data labelling — not competing with your employer's business. We recommend you review your employment agreement. We sign NDAs with all QEN members to protect confidentiality on both sides.

How is quality measured, and how does my tier advance?+

We track four metrics: agreement rate (do your responses align with peer reviewers?), completion rate, on-time rate, and flag accuracy (for audit tasks). Your tier advances automatically when you cross quality thresholds — there's no application process.

What types of tasks will I see as a Tier 1 Contributor?+

Dataset labelling and annotation, AI response rating (RLHF — rating pairs of model outputs), red-team prompt creation, and policy pack feedback. Tasks come with clear rubrics and pay rates stated upfront. You only accept tasks you want.

Can I join if I'm outside India?+

Yes. QEN is open to experts globally. We have particular demand for EU AI Act specialists, UK FCA and ICO-familiar practitioners, and specialists in Middle East AI regulation. Payments are in INR for India-based contributors and USD/USDC for international members.

What makes QEN different from other freelance platforms?+

QEN tasks require genuine domain expertise — not generic annotation. The work directly shapes enterprise AI governance products and real client outcomes. And unlike anonymous freelance platforms, QEN offers named contribution credits, co-authorship on research outputs, and a path to Qapitol Labs fellowship.
