FAQ

Common questions

Is the Free plan really free forever? +
Yes. The QAVE Free plan is permanently free — no credit card required, no time limit. You get 500 eval runs per month and access to core compliance frameworks. If you outgrow it, upgrading is instant and you keep your entire eval history.
Can I try Enterprise features before committing? +
Absolutely. We offer a 14-day Enterprise trial for qualifying teams, including Sovereign AI deployment, full SLM access, and all regulatory frameworks. Contact our sales team — typically set up within 48 hours.
What does “Sovereign AI” mean in practice? +
Sovereign AI means your data, models, evaluation workloads, and audit trails never leave your environment. We deploy inside your AWS, Azure, or GCP VPC — or fully air-gapped on-prem. No API calls to Qapitol’s cloud. This satisfies RBI data localization, IRDAI data residency, and DPDP Act obligations for regulated entities.
How does managed services pricing work? +
Services engagements are scoped individually based on outcomes, team composition, and timelines. We offer fixed-price models (compliance assessments, platform implementation) and T&M (ongoing delivery, staff augmentation). Every engagement starts with a free scoping call before you commit.
Do you offer startup discounts? +
Yes — our Startup Program offers up to 40% off Growth and Platform plans for pre-Series B companies. We evaluate based on stage and team size, not just funding. Mention “Startup Program” in your contact form.
Can CHEQ and QAVE work together? +
They’re designed to work together. QAVE runs the AI evaluations; CHEQ maps results against regulatory obligations and generates the evidence trail. On Professional plan and above, CHEQ pulls eval data directly from QAVE — creating a closed loop from model evaluation to compliance reporting.
Do you have USD/AED pricing for international clients? +
Yes. Prices shown are in INR for India-based clients. International clients are billed in USD (USA, UK, Europe) or AED (UAE). Roughly: QAVE Growth at $299/mo, CHEQ Starter at $599/mo. Enterprise pricing is always custom regardless of currency.
