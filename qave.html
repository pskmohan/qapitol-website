Why it exists

AI systems break in ways you didn’t plan for.

Hallucinations. Inconsistent answers. Unsafe outputs. Edge cases nobody thought to write a test for. The job of QAVE is to plan for them — to generate the scenarios, simulate the user behaviour, and surface the failure modes before your customers do.

The Independence Problem

OpenAI is not incentivized to tell you
your model is underperforming. QAVE is.

Every enterprise that deploys AI through Azure OpenAI, AWS Bedrock, Google Vertex, or any foundation model provider faces the same structural conflict: the vendor who sold you the model is not the right entity to evaluate whether it’s failing your users.

⚖
The Auditor Problem Applied to AI Evaluation
“Would you trust an auditor employed by the company being audited? The structural conflict is identical. OpenAI builds the model — OpenAI is not incentivized to surface its own model’s failures. Microsoft sells you Azure OpenAI — Microsoft is not incentivized to tell you when it underperforms your domain expectations. AWS builds Bedrock — AWS is not incentivized to benchmark Claude or Titan against your specific use case and call out drift. The model vendor and the eval layer cannot be the same entity.”
QAVE = the independent assurance layer for AI deployed through any model provider or hyperscaler stack.

What it does

Simulation-driven AI validation.

Six capability layers that take you from chaos simulation to deployment-ready confidence.

Adversarial Inputs

Adversarial scenario generation

Edge cases, boundary inputs, and adversarial prompts generated systematically by role and risk category. Covers injection attacks, semantic drift, context manipulation, and domain-specific failure patterns your team hasn’t thought to write yet.

What you get: 500+ scenario templates across 12 risk dimensions.
Know more →
User Simulation

Multi-turn conversation simulation

Realistic user journeys across 40+ persona archetypes. Journey stress testing and context drift detection expose how your AI degrades across extended interactions — the kind of failure only visible after turn 8 of a customer support conversation.

What you get: Journey libraries for BFSI, Healthcare, SaaS, and Retail.
Know more →
Failure Detection

Failure mode detection

Hallucination scoring, inconsistency detection, unsafe output flagging, and refusal rate analysis run simultaneously across your eval suite. Each classifier is tunable to your risk tolerance — tighter thresholds for regulated domains, wider for exploratory products.

What you get: 18 failure mode classifiers with tunable thresholds.
Know more →
Scoring Engine

Evaluation scoring engine

Multi-metric scoring across accuracy, relevance, faithfulness, safety, and consistency. LLM-as-judge and rule-based scoring run side by side so you can compare verdicts, catch judge bias, and build a trustworthy score you can defend to a regulator or product lead.

What you get: Pluggable metric registry. Bring your own judge model.
Know more →
Data Management

Dataset management

Versioned eval datasets, ground truth management, and annotation workflows purpose-built for AI evaluation cycles. Track which dataset version was used for every eval run so regressions are traceable and audit requests are answerable in minutes, not days.

What you get: Native HuggingFace, S3, and local dataset connectors.
Know more →
CI/CD Integration

CI/CD integration & eval gates

GitHub Actions, Jenkins, and GitLab CI integrations ship eval gates directly into your deployment pipeline. Block releases that fail quality thresholds before a single user is exposed. Configurable per environment so staging gates are stricter than canary.

What you get: Fail-fast eval gates with configurable thresholds.

Regulatory coverage

One eval engine. The frameworks that matter in your market.

QAVE evaluates against regulatory standards and responsible AI benchmarks. Coverage expands via new framework ingestion sprints.

Insurance / India

IRDAI AI Guidelines

Evaluates AI outputs against IRDAI guidance for automated decision-making in insurance products, customer communication, and underwriting systems.

EU Regulation

EU AI Act High-Risk

Conformity assessment evaluation for high-risk AI systems under the EU AI Act. Covers transparency, accuracy, robustness, and human oversight requirements.

Banking / India

RBI AI Framework

Evaluation against RBI guidelines for AI use in banking — explainability, data localisation, model risk management, and audit trail standards.

Financial Services / EU

DORA

Digital Operational Resilience Act evaluation covering AI-dependent operational processes, ICT risk management, and incident reporting obligations.

Insurance / EU

Solvency II

Evaluation of AI models used in solvency calculations, risk modelling, and capital adequacy reporting for EU insurance entities.

Custom Frameworks

Custom eval suites

Define your own evaluation criteria, metric weights, and pass/fail thresholds. Map internal AI policy documents to structured eval rules QAVE can run automatically.

AI Ethics

Responsible AI benchmarks

Fairness, bias, and equity evaluation across demographic dimensions. Evaluation against published responsible AI benchmarks including BBQ, WinoBias, and ToxiGen.

Model Safety

Safety & alignment eval

Red-team style safety evaluation — jailbreak resistance, prompt injection detection, refusal rate analysis, and alignment with your system prompt instructions under adversarial pressure.
