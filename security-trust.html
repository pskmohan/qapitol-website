Data Handling

Exactly what we do — and don't do — with your data.

We process only the data you bring to us. We do not train on client data, do not retain outputs beyond the agreed session scope, and do not share data with third parties except listed sub-processors.

Every data processing engagement begins with a signed Data Processing Agreement (DPA). Sub-processor changes are communicated with 30 days notice. Client data is purged within 30 days of contract end, with written confirmation provided.

Contractual guarantee

No-train policy is explicitly stated in our Master Services Agreement. We will not use your data to train, fine-tune, or benchmark any model — Qapitol's or third-party.

Key data protection facts

AES-256 at rest TLS 1.3 in transit Mumbai AWS region (India) No cross-client data sharing Data deleted in 30 days of contract end Sub-processor list on request DPA executed before any processing No model training on client data Output retention: session scope only

Incident Response

When something goes wrong, here's exactly what happens.

Our incident response process is documented, practiced, and available to enterprise clients on request.

1
2-hour SLA

Detection & Internal Triage

Security monitoring systems trigger an alert. Internal triage begins within 2 hours. Incident severity classified as P1 (Critical), P2 (High), P3 (Medium), or P4 (Low).

2
4-hour SLA for P1

Client Notification

Affected clients notified within 4 hours of confirming a P1 incident. Notification includes: what we know, what we don't know, immediate containment actions taken, and next update ETA.

3
24h critical / 72h high

Resolution

Target resolution is 24 hours for critical severity incidents, 72 hours for high. Containment, eradication, and recovery steps documented throughout.

4
5 business days

Post-Incident Review & RCA

Written Root Cause Analysis delivered within 5 business days. Includes what happened, root cause, what we changed, and timeline of events. Shared with affected clients.

CISO Escalation Contact

Enterprise clients have a dedicated security escalation contact for P1 incidents. Not a generic inbox — a named contact who knows your environment.
