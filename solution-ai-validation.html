The Validation Gap

Passing CI/CD is not the same as validated AI.

Traditional software testing checks whether your code runs. AI system validation checks whether your model reasons correctly — under adversarial conditions, across edge cases, with real user inputs that deviate from your training distribution.

Without AI System Validation
✗ Model passes tests on your dataset; fails on user inputs you didn't anticipate
✗ No baseline — you can't tell if a model update improved or regressed performance
✗ Regulatory compliance is assumed, never verified against specific obligations
✗ Safety failures discovered by customers or auditors, not your QA process
✗ Model drift goes undetected until business metrics crater
