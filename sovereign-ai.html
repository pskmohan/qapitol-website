The Problem

Hyperscalers Can't Give You This

Cloud AI APIs route your enterprise data through foreign infrastructure, training pipelines, and abuse-detection systems you don't control. Regulators in India, UAE, EU, and the US federal government are closing that window.

🏛️

Regulatory Mandate

DPDP, RBI IT Framework, IRDAI, SEBI, UAE PDPL, ADGM, and EU AI Act all impose data localisation requirements on AI workloads handling personal or regulated data.

🛡️

National Security

Defence, intelligence, and critical infrastructure organisations cannot use shared-cloud inference. Prompts, completions, and model weights must stay air-gapped.

📋

Audit Traceability

Regulators require complete logs of every AI decision, every prompt, and every output. Public LLM APIs offer none of this. On-premise gives you full audit sovereignty.

⚡

Latency & Reliability

Mission-critical AI — trading, clinical decision support, real-time fraud — cannot tolerate external API latency or third-party SLA dependencies.

💰

Cost at Scale

At 10M+ tokens/day, cloud API costs compound rapidly. On-premise open-weight models (Llama 3, Mistral, Phi-4) reduce inference cost by 60–85% vs GPT-4 class APIs.

🔑

IP & Model Ownership

Fine-tuned models trained on your proprietary data belong to you — not a hyperscaler. Sovereign deployment ensures your AI competitive advantage stays proprietary.

Reference Architecture

The Qapitol Sovereign AI Stack

A fully integrated on-premise AI deployment — from model hosting to governance audit trail — built on open-weight models and your existing cloud or on-prem infrastructure.

Deployment Layers

Layer 1

Model Hosting & Inference

vLLM / TGI serving on your GPU cluster (NVIDIA A100/H100 or AMD MI300X). Supports Llama 3.1-70B/405B, Mistral Large, Phi-4, Qwen2.5, Falcon — fully air-gapped.

Layer 2

Evaluation & Safety Gate (QAVE)

Every prompt and completion passes through SURE-Q evaluation before reaching the application layer. Bias, toxicity, hallucination, and policy compliance scored in real time.

Layer 3

Compliance & Audit Layer (CHEQ)

Every AI decision is logged, timestamped, and mapped to regulatory obligations. IRDAI, RBI, EU AI Act, and DPDP audit reports generated automatically.

Layer 4

Orchestration (Nexus / Agent Fabric)

Multi-agent workflows, RAG pipelines, and tool-use orchestration — all running within your perimeter. Integrates with your existing data warehouse and API layer.

Deployment Options

Option A

Private Cloud (VPC-Isolated)

Deploy on your existing AWS/Azure/GCP account in a dedicated VPC with no external egress. Data residency in your chosen region. Fastest time to production.

Option B

On-Premise Data Centre

Full hardware provisioning and model deployment on your physical infrastructure. Fully offline capability — suitable for banks, regulators, and government agencies.

Option C

Air-Gapped / SCIFs

Fully disconnected deployment for defence, intelligence, and critical national infrastructure. No internet connectivity required at any layer. Delivered via secure media transfer.
