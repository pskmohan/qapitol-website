The SURE-Q Framework

AI confidence isn’t a destination.
It’s a framework.

SURE-Q is Qapitol’s Business Outcome Assurance methodology — five interconnected disciplines that take you from evaluating AI through securing it, deploying it at scale, relying on it in production, and continuously evolving it. This is how enterprise AI stops being a cost centre and starts delivering provable business value.

Five Pillars

Every letter earns its place.

SURE-Q isn’t a checklist. It’s a lifecycle — five disciplines that work independently and exponentially better together.

Q
Qualify
Evaluate & Baseline AI

Before you trust AI, you must understand it. SURE-Q Qualify gives you the methodology, platforms, and expert practitioners to evaluate any AI system — foundation models, agentic pipelines, or custom LLMs — against rigorous, domain-specific benchmarks. You can’t manage what you haven’t measured.

Services:
AI Evaluation (Evals) · Agentic QE · Legacy Modernisation Testing

Secure
Compliance & Governance

Regulatory frameworks for AI are no longer optional — they’re existential. SURE-Q Secure maps your AI systems to EU AI Act requirements, ISO 42001, IRDAI, RBI, and sector-specific mandates through CHEQ, our AI compliance platform. Stay ahead of regulators, not behind them.

Platforms: CHEQ Nexus
Services:
Security Testing · Consulting & Advisory · AI Governance Frameworks

Put AI to Work

Evaluation and governance are only valuable if AI actually does something for your business. SURE-Q Utilise is where we deploy AI agents, build automation workflows, and integrate AI into your engineering and business processes — with oversight built in from day one.

AI Workflow Automation · Agentic Automation · Process Automation

SURE-Q Works For

Built for where you actually are.

SURE-Q isn’t one-size-fits-all. Different organisations need different pillars at different intensities — here’s how it maps to the three most common AI journeys.

🚀

Startups Scaling AI Fast

You’re moving quickly, building on foundation models, and need AI that works reliably before you hit regulatory scrutiny. The priority is getting AI qualified and into production with confidence — not getting buried in compliance overhead you don’t need yet.

Q — Qualify U — Utilise E — Evolve
🏛️

Enterprises Governing AI at Scale

You have AI in production but are facing increasing regulatory pressure and board-level scrutiny. You need governance infrastructure, continuous monitoring, and the ability to demonstrate compliance — to regulators, to auditors, and to your own leadership.

S — Secure R — Rely Q — Qualify
🌏

GCCs Building AI Capability

You’re establishing or scaling a Global Capability Centre with a mandate to build AI competency for your parent organisation. You need the full spectrum — from talent and tooling to governance and delivery frameworks — and you need it to be production-grade from day one.

All 5 Pillars E — Evolve
Analyst Framework Alignment

SURE-Q maps 3 of 4 Gartner AI TRiSM layers today — the fourth is in development.

Gartner's AI Trust, Risk and Security Management (AI TRiSM) framework defines four layers every enterprise must address before deploying AI at scale. SURE-Q gives Qapitol managed coverage of Layers 1, 2, and 3 — and we're actively building Layer 4 (Infrastructure & Model Security) into the service.

Where most vendors offer a point tool for one layer, Qapitol provides a unified managed service that spans governance, runtime quality, and information integrity — with model-level security coming next. One partner, one accountability contract, all four layers.
