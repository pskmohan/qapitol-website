Pricing Model Clarity

Three ways to work with us on talent

No opaque pricing. Here's exactly how each model works — so you can choose before the first call.

No Upfront Cost

Contingency

No upfront cost. You only pay a placement fee when a candidate successfully joins — no success, no fee. Includes a 12-week guarantee with free replacement if the hire doesn't work out.

  • Fee on successful placement only
  • 12-week guarantee — free replacement
  • No retainer, no minimum commitment

Best for: Permanent hires, up to 3 simultaneous roles.

Dedicated Partner

Retained Search

A dedicated talent partner works exclusively on your brief. You get priority bench access, weekly pipeline reports, and a committed sourcing resource — not one of thirty briefs in a queue.

  • Dedicated talent partner assigned
  • Priority access to the full bench
  • Weekly pipeline reports

Best for: Urgent, specialist, or multiple simultaneous hires.

Ongoing Supply

RPO (Recruitment Process Outsourcing)

Qapitol runs your entire AI/ML hiring function end-to-end on a monthly retainer. We source, screen, schedule, and manage pipeline — you just interview and decide.

  • Monthly retainer model
  • Ongoing talent supply — no per-hire fees
  • GCC buildout support included

Best for: GCC buildouts or sustained hiring (10+ roles/year).
